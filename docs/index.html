Sirocco IPTV

A player for your own IPTV subscription, on Roku.

Sirocco IPTV turns your Roku into a clean, fast player for the IPTV service you already subscribe to. Enter your provider's server, username and password — or paste your M3U link — and you're in.

  • Live TV with categories, channel numbers you can type on the remote, and now/next programme info when your provider supplies it
  • Movies and series with detail pages, season lists, and resume where you left off
  • Favorites across live, movies and series, with their own row on Home
  • Search your whole catalogue
  • Parental control — lock any category behind a PIN
  • Multiple providers, saved and switched with one click
  • Catch-up for channels whose provider offers an archive

Install

Sirocco IPTV is currently distributed as a Roku beta app. Add it to your Roku account with the access code, then it appears on your device after a restart or a manual update check.

my.roku.com/account/add/SIROCCO  ·  access code SIROCCO

What you need

An active subscription with an Xtream Codes–compatible IPTV provider. Sirocco IPTV does not include any channels or content; it only plays what your provider gives you. We have no relationship with any provider.

What we don't do

No ads. No accounts with us. No tracking. Your credentials are stored on your Roku and sent only to the server you configured. Details in the privacy policy.

Support

Something not working? Write to ahmed@goliaths.io. Include your Roku model and, if it's a playback problem, the name of the channel or title.

Most playback failures are the provider's stream rather than the app: a channel that has gone offline, or a file in a format Roku cannot decode (AVI, DTS audio, MPEG-2 video). The app hides what it has found to be unavailable and tells you when a format is unsupported.
