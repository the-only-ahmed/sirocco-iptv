Sirocco IPTV / Privacy policy

Privacy Policy

Effective 7 September 2026

Sirocco IPTV is a player for Xtream Codes–compatible IPTV services. It does not provide any content. It connects to the server you configure, using the account you hold with that provider.

What the app stores, and where

Everything Sirocco IPTV saves is kept on your Roku device only:

  • the server address, username and password for each provider you add;
  • your favorites, the position you stopped watching at, and channels the app has found to be unavailable;
  • your parental-control PIN and the categories you have locked;
  • a temporary cache of channel and catalogue lists, so they open faster next time.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. We do not run any servers, and the app never contacts one of ours.

What the app sends, and to whom

The app talks to exactly one party: the IPTV server you entered. To do that it sends that server your username and password, as the Xtream Codes protocol requires. What that provider does with your data is governed by their privacy policy, not this one.

What we do not do

No analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, no tracking, no third-party services. We collect nothing about you, and we have nothing to sell or share.

Roku

Roku, Inc. may collect usage data about apps on its platform under its own privacy policy, which we do not control.

Deleting your data

Remove a saved server from the login screen, or use the clear options in Settings. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.

Children

The app is not directed at children. Content is supplied by your provider and is not rated by us; the parental-control PIN is provided for that reason.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
